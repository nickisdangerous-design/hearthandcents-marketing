What's inside

Everything your household needs, nothing it doesn't.

Five modules that talk to each other — designed around how families actually plan, spend, and live.

Budget & Cashflow

Envelope-style spending categories, automatic bank transaction sync via email, and a visual cashflow map showing exactly where your money goes — income in, savings and transfers out, discretionary spend at a glance.

Meal Planning

AI-generated weekly dinner plans built around your household's dietary restrictions, preferred stores, and 30-minute prep limit. One tap for a full week. Swap any night from an alternatives bench without another AI call.

Smart Shopping

Ingredients flow straight from your meal plan into per-store shopping lists. King Soopers, Walmart, Costco, Whole Foods — each list routes to the right store. Open pickup directly from the app.

Household To-Dos

A shared task board for everything that doesn't fit anywhere else. Assign to a person, set priority, and track what's open — without switching to yet another app.

Maintenance Tracking

Recurring household tasks — oil changes, cleaning schedules, service appointments — tracked automatically, surfaced when due, and dismissed when done.

Private by Design

Google sign-in, secure sessions, and a privacy mode that blurs financial figures on screen. Your household's data stays yours. No advertising. No data mining. No surprises.

How it works

From bank email to dinner table in minutes.

01
Connect your bank email

Link your Gmail and we watch for transaction emails from your bank. Purchases are parsed, categorized automatically, and appear in your budget — no manual entry, no CSV imports, no third-party bank credentials.

02
Generate your week of meals

Tell us your dietary needs, preferred stores, and time constraints once. Every week, one tap generates a full dinner plan tailored to your household. Swap any meal from the idea bench, or drag dinners between days.

03
Build your shopping carts

Ingredients sort by store automatically. Check off what you already have, add anything extra, then open pickup directly from the app. Separate, clean lists for every store you use.

04
Watch the picture form

Spending flows into your budget, your cashflow map updates, maintenance gets tracked. The whole household runs a little more smoothly each week — without extra effort from you.
